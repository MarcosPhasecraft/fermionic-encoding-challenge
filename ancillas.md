A third challenge, structurally different from the [square-lattice](../) and
[graph](../graphs/) leaderboards above: submissions here are *expected* to use
ancilla qubits and a real stabilizer group. The maximum Pauli weight is fixed
at 3, and the goal is to minimize the number of ancilla qubits needed to
reach a genuinely valid encoding at that weight. Full rules, methodology, and
how to submit an encoding: see the
[GitHub repo](https://github.com/MarcosPhasecraft/fermionic-encoding-challenge).
