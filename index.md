Live progress on finding the best fermion-to-qubit encoding for 2D lattices,
scored on total and maximum Pauli weight. Full rules, methodology, and how
to submit an encoding: see the
[GitHub repo](https://github.com/MarcosPhasecraft/fermionic-encoding-challenge).
There's also a [separate challenge](graphs/) for non-square 2D lattices
(hexagonal, triangular, and their periodic variants), and a
[third challenge](ancillas/) for ancilla/stabilizer encodings.
